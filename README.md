# Freedom Travel for Cursor

Live flights and hotels for Kazakhstan, Central Asia and the CIS, right in Cursor's agent. Search, compare and get a booking link on [freedom-travel.kz](https://freedom-travel.kz).

Freedom Travel is the travel arm of Freedom Holding (NASDAQ: FRHC): $600M+ B2C travel turnover and 5.4M air and rail tickets a year.

## What the plugin does

The plugin connects Cursor to the Freedom Travel MCP server at `https://api.freedom-travel.kz/mcp`. Your agent can:

- search one-way and round-trip flights for up to 9 passengers, with filters for stops, airlines, times and price;
- compare fares of a flight: price, checked baggage, hand luggage, refund and exchange;
- show a price calendar for a route;
- search hotels by city, dates and guests, and show details, reviews and live room rates;
- give you a booking link for the chosen flight or hotel.

Results are labelled cheapest, fastest and most convenient. Prices are live, in Kazakhstani tenge (KZT).

## Sign in

1. Install the plugin from the Cursor Marketplace.
2. Open Cursor Settings → MCP. Next to **freedom-travel**, select **Connect**.
3. A browser window opens with Freedom Travel sign-in. Sign in with your Freedom Travel account (phone number or email) and allow access for Cursor.
4. Back in Cursor, the server shows as connected. Ask the agent, for example: "Find direct flights from Almaty to Istanbul next month for one adult."

To sign out, select **Disconnect** next to the server in Settings → MCP.

## Try it

- Find direct flights from Almaty to Istanbul next month for one adult.
- When is it cheapest to fly from Almaty to Dubai next month?
- Find a 4-star hotel in Tashkent with breakfast for two adults, 3 nights next week.
- How much is the first flight with checked baggage? Is there a refundable fare?

The plugin connects to one service, `https://api.freedom-travel.kz/mcp`, operated by Freedom Travel (TOO Aviata, Almaty, Kazakhstan). Booking and payment happen on freedom-travel.kz.
